# Brewline landing page

A one-page marketing site for Brewline, a small coffee roaster that sells a monthly bean subscription.

## Who uses it

People who find Brewline online and want to know what the subscription includes before they sign up.

## What they can do

- Read a short hero section with the product name, one sentence about the subscription, and a "Join the waitlist" button.
- See three plans in a pricing section: Solo (1 bag, $18/month), Duo (2 bags, $32/month), and Office (5 bags, $70/month).
- Read four answers in a FAQ section: shipping, pausing, cancelling, and roast dates.
- Enter an email in a waitlist form. The form checks the email format and shows a thank-you message. It does not send the email anywhere.

## Constraints

- One page at `/`. No backend, no database, no login.
- It must work on a phone and on a desktop.
