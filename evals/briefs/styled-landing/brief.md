# Tidewell landing page

A one-page marketing site for Tidewell, a smart ring that tracks sleep and tells you each morning how rested you are.

## Who uses it

People aged 25 to 45 who sleep badly, read reviews before they buy gadgets, and compare Tidewell with other sleep trackers.

## What they can do

- See a hero with the product name, one sentence about better sleep, the ring itself, and a "Pre-order for $249" button.
- Read how it works in three steps: wear it, sleep, read your morning score.
- See the specs: 7-day battery, water resistant to 100 m, titanium, 4 sizes.
- Read five answers in a FAQ section: sizing, battery, subscription (none), privacy, and returns.
- Enter an email to pre-order. The form checks the email format and shows a confirmation. It does not send the email anywhere.

## Constraints

- One page at `/`. No backend, no database, no login.
- It must work on a phone and on a desktop.
- The page should feel premium and calm, not like a generic tech template.
